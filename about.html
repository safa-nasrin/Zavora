About Us

Zavora is a shopping discovery website. We pick products across fashion, beauty, home and lifestyle so you can find good options faster.

How it works

We are not an online store. We do not sell, ship or take payments. When you click "Shop Now", you go to a partner store (such as Flipkart), where you view the latest price and buy the product.

How we earn

We take part in affiliate programs of partner stores, including the Flipkart Affiliate Program. If you buy through our links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Read our Affiliate Disclosure.

Our promise

We only recommend products we think are useful, and we will be clear about what is sponsored or affiliate-linked.
